## How to get started

Test your apps over the web in three simple steps
![](https://d3unf4s5rp9dfh.cloudfront.net/REMOTETESTLAB/remote-test-lab-img-1.png)

#### Step 1. Sign up - it’s free!

Not a member? Sign up now.
![](https://d3unf4s5rp9dfh.cloudfront.net/REMOTETESTLAB/remote-test-lab-img-2.png)

#### Step 2. Select your preferences

To learn more about using Remote Test Lab, refer to the developer documentation.
![](https://d3unf4s5rp9dfh.cloudfront.net/REMOTETESTLAB/remote-test-lab-img-3.png)

#### Step 3. Start Testing

Go to the Remote Test Lab page, and click START TESTING.
